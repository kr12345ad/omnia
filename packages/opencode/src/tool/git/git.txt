Inspect and manage the repository's version control state.

Run this before and after editing so you know what you changed and what was
already there. It returns parsed results, not raw terminal output.

Read-only operations (no permission prompt):
- status: branch, upstream, and changed files with their staged state.
- diff: the current changes. Set staged to compare the index against HEAD,
  and paths to limit the output.
- log: recent commits with their files. Narrow with limit, path, or grep.
- show: one commit's full patch, by its id or a ref like HEAD~2.
- blame: who last touched each line of a file, optionally a line range.
- branch: list branches, or create/switch with create/switch.

Mutating operations (ask the user first):
- add: stage paths, or everything with all.
- restore: discard changes. Unstage with staged, or discard edits with worktree.
- stash: shelve changes, or bring them back with pop.

Always check status first: it separates your edits from work that was already in
the tree, so you do not report someone else's change as your own.
